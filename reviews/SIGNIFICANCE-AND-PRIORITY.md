## 1. One precise theorem to put forward

For every integer n>=5, every finite dimension, and every real symmetric or complex Hermitian tuple satisfying

    A_i >= 0,                 sum_i A_i <= n I,

let

    W_5,n = (1/(n)_5) sum_(i1,...,i5 all distinct) A_i1 A_i2 A_i3 A_i4 A_i5.

Define

    r_n = n/(n-1),
    Delta_n = sqrt(20n^2-60n+49),
    a_n = n(6n-9+Delta_n)/(16(n-1)(n-2)),
    kappa_5(n) = a_n^2 [r_n(3-r_n)a_n - r_n^2/2].

The selected claim is

    W_5,n >= -kappa_5(n) I,

with equality on a nonzero vector for a real n-dimensional tuple for every n>=5. Consequently kappa_5(n) is the exact dimension-unrestricted lower constant. The ordered average, rather than the unordered subset normalization, is essential.

This includes the previously isolated endpoint

    kappa_5(5) = [3125(9033+581 sqrt(249))/393216]/120

and has the exact asymptotic value

    lim_(n->infinity) kappa_5(n) = (11+5 sqrt(5))/64.

These expressions come from the campaign's already reviewed elementary optimization of the known family recurrence. The new mathematical content, if accepted, is the matching universal inequality on the full subunital domain for all n, not the optimized family formula or the existence of its witness.

## 2. The original question and the precise extent of resolution

In the published ICML/PMLR version of **Lai and Lim, “Recht–Ré Noncommutative Arithmetic-Geometric Mean Conjecture is False” (2020)**, §6, PDF page 9, the authors ask:

> “What is the correct lower bound?”

The surrounding paragraph distinguishes the overly negative lower estimates at lengths 2 and 3 from the inadequate magnitude at length 5. A sharp all-n five-factor theorem answers that question completely **for m=5**, including the known failing endpoint and the entire subsequent n-range. It does not answer the question for arbitrary m. [Published primary PDF, §6](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/lai20a/lai20a.pdf).

The same paper's Table 1 already reports the numerical raw-sum endpoint 144.6488 at (m,n)=(5,5). Its initial disproof is prior work. The proposed contribution converts the family value into a universal, exact, attained formula for infinitely many n; it does not rediscover the failure or claim the original Recht–Ré conjecture is newly disproved. [Published article and version](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/lai20a.html).

Why choose this over another bound? It determines the extremal quantity, with the reverse inequality supplied by an explicit attaining family, over every admissible matrix dimension and every integer n in a whole product-length class. This is stronger evidence of resolving a mathematical question than a local SDP optimum, finite numerical sweep, or a nonsharp restoration threshold. Calling it a substantial standalone computer-assisted theorem is defensible if acceptance and priority checks close. Calling it a general solution of NCAGM, a newly proved superiority of random reshuffling, or a guaranteed hiring outcome is not.
